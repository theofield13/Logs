## Module 1 - Getting started on Youtube

### most common mistake beginners make

picking a niche that is already saturated or dying


### niches to avoid

- never do politics (YT hate individuals doing this)
- crime niche (too hard)
- AI stories (YT will crack down on those prob)
- horror stories (too hard)
- police body cam footage

### how to find video topics for your channel

pick topics that are max 1-2 weeks old since trends are alive for usually 3 weeks

### how to copy competitors

you want to be some similar that viewers won't notice their on the 'wrong' channel

### When to expect results... 

you need enough data: 
- 15 for rending niches, 10 for evergreen
- did any of those 10 (everegreen) get > 200 views => you are NOT shadowbanned. For trending niche it's 500 views
- 4-5 videos per week is the minimum 
- if you can post 30 days per month, you have 30 tries to get one banger. 1M views => 7-10k $ 
- we are NOT content creators. Focus on the main levers -- the packaging which is the video title and thumbnail.

There is a lot of dogshit videos on YT. You only have to make great title and thumbnial and get quality of video barely above dogshit-level.

You have 30 tries per month.

## Module 2 - Content planning strategy

### How to easily double scripts 

If a topic is super-hot you might want to create several videos on said topics. You can re-use a video by using these two hacks:

Go to the script of said video and 
1. re-arrange the different parts
2. re-write the introduction 

### How to choose my video tags

VidiQ is recommended - just use the ones coming up on the side of the video

They should look like these:

times celebs clapped back at ellen, entertainment, celeb news, top 10, ellen

### how to come up with video titles 

- always capitalize the words in the title
- if you're doing a "top 10 xx" => "10 times xx .." and you can also freshen it up with 2026, more, new etc..
- always start video ideas with title and thumbnail
